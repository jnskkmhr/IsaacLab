* Added tracking camera support to the Newton GL and Newton RTX visualizers. **Camera View** lists it as "Tracking Camera" beside the
  free "Perspective" view, and a Tracking Camera group edits its environment, asset, body, eye, look-at, focal length
  and heading settings, with a reset to the task's declaration. Recording updated the tracking pose on demand,
  including for unselected cameras and headless capture.
